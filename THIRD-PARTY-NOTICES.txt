Fonts
   BulkPrint Manager does not bundle fonts. Documents are drawn with the fonts
   installed in Windows.


Faithful output
   Office documents are converted by LibreOffice, not by Microsoft Office. Most
   documents print as expected, but complex layouts, uncommon fonts, macros or
   embedded objects can differ from Microsoft Office. Use Preview to check
   important documents. If the Office engine is not installed, Word, Excel and
   PowerPoint files are marked "Cannot print" and are never printed incorrectly.
